Austin's Weekly Premium Cinema Schedule

Your free guide to premium & speciality screenings in Austin designed for cinema lovers. Available as a daily or weekly email, and on the Cinemaxxing.co website!

We're freaks for the cinema and Austin has a surplus of fantastic films to see on any given day. But it can be hard to keep up and make sure you don't miss that new 35mm screening or 4K restoration you heard was coming months ago. Cinemaxxing curates all of these listings and sends them to your inbox (daily or weekly) and will improve your moviegoing experiences. Because when you maximize your cinema intake, you maximize your life!
