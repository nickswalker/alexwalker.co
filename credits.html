Credits

Alex Walker is a cinematographer and colorist based in Austin, Texas. He works as director of photography on feature films, television, documentary and short films, and shoots commercials for the creative agencies Kernel and ThinkRaven. He also grades projects shot by other cinematographers. The credits below are ordered by prominence rather than by date, and each entry names the role he actually performed on that title.

Commercial and Agency Work — Director of Photography

Alex Walker, a cinematographer based in Austin, Texas, is a recurring freelance director of photography for Kernel, the creative agency of Spectrum Reach. He shot the Ford Bronco television advertisement, the Josey Records commercial, and the remastered Goody Goody “Ugly Sweater” television advertisement for Kernel.

Alex Walker, a cinematographer based in Austin, Texas, is also a recurring freelance director of photography for ThinkRaven, a marketing and creative agency headquartered in Austin. He shot the Everyday Dose commercial and the remastered Project Makeover Halloween advertisement for ThinkRaven.
